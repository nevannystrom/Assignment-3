# Assignment-3

## Exercise Descriptions

### Exercise 1 – Retail Checkout Simulation
The program asks the user to enter item prices until 0 is entered, stores the prices in a list, and calculates the total purchase amount, average item cost, and number of items bought.

### Exercise 2 – Market Survey Analyzer
The program analyzes a list of customer product preferences, counts how many times each product is chosen, and prints the market-share percentage for each product.

### Exercise 3 – Expense Report Categorizer
The program uses a dictionary of expense categories and amounts, calculates totals for each category, and computes the overall expense total.

### Exercise 4 – Sales Commission Calculator
The program calculates a 10% commission for each employee based on sales data and prints a ranked leaderboard of commissions earned.

### Exercise 5 – Stock Portfolio Tracker
The program calculates the total value of a stock portfolio based on shares and prices, and simulates daily price changes over a week.

### Exercise 6 – Bank Loan Repayment Simulator
The program asks for loan details and simulates monthly payments using a loop to determine how many months are required to pay off the loan.

### Exercise 7 – Simple Supply Chain Tracker
The program loops through a list of warehouses and inventory dictionaries to calculate the total quantity of each product across all warehouses.

### Exercise 8 – Customer Loyalty Tiers
The program classifies customers into Bronze, Silver, and Gold tiers based on total purchase amounts and counts how many customers fall into each tier.

### Exercise 9 – Business Growth Projection
The program projects business revenue growth over 10 years using a loop and a growth rate.

### Exercise 10 – Startup Pitch Deck Visualizer
The program prints an ASCII bar chart representing projected revenue values using the `#` character.
